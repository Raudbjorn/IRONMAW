# IRONMAW

**Wormian Bones**, in everyday use. *I am worn bones.*

A system of uncommon parts: charcoal, teal, rust and slate for the Application
Assistant library and reader. Wormian bones are accessory bones inside the
skull's sutures — unexpected pieces within an ordered structure. The system
takes that literally: fitted shapes, visible seams, carefully differentiated
parts. No skull illustrations.

## Three names, one letter set

| On paper | Day to day | Things of the theme |
|---|---|---|
| **IRONMAW** | IRONMAW or **WORMIAN BONES** | **Wormian** |

IRONMAW ⇄ WORMIAN is an exact anagram; IRONMAW is the bear, the heavier,
more animal side of the same design.

## Editions

- **Oxide** — the charcoal, teal, rust and slate expression. Default; `data-theme="oxide"`.
- **Bone** — a proposed paper-toned expression for documents and reading. Opt-in; `data-theme="bone"`.

## Rules the components enforce

- Teal is interactive emphasis: focus, selection, filter state, links.
- Rust is the main action and the active indicator — one filled control per view.
- Warnings are amber, never rust. A notice must not look like an action.
- Display lettering (Rajdhani, uppercase, tracked) is for short labels only. Titles, excerpts, warnings and Icelandic prose stay in Inter sentence case.
- Review labels have three tones: act (filled), care (rust outline), context (slate outline). No green, no "Verified".
- The chamfer is one size; the suture is the only ornament.
